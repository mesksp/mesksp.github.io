Vision-Based Manipulation Dual-Arm Manipulation Robot Learning Reinforcement Learning Motion Planning Embodied AI

My research builds robots that perceive, plan, and act with the dexterity needed for the real world. I develop vision-driven control and learning methods for single- and dual-arm manipulators — uniting perception, motion planning, and control into deployable systems, and validating them at scale in high-fidelity simulation with NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab before transferring them to physical hardware.
